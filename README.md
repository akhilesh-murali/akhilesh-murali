## How I work

1. **Understand the problem** — Clarify the business goal, users, requirements, and constraints.
2. **Define the scope** — Agree on deliverables, milestones, timeline, and budget before development.
3. **Build iteratively** — Share progress, test key workflows, and gather feedback.
4. **Deliver properly** — Provide the agreed code, deployment, documentation, and handover.

I value clear communication, maintainable code, practical solutions, and realistic commitments.

## Who I work with

- **Startup founders** who need an MVP or a new product feature.
- **Small businesses** looking to replace repetitive manual processes with software.
- **Software agencies** that need help with backend development, integrations, or overflow projects.
- **Teams with existing applications** that need bug fixes, improvements, or new functionality.

I’m open to both short, clearly scoped projects and longer-term development partnerships.
